    Update notes for MadGraph7 (in reverse time order)

ANNOUNCEMENT: 
   The 2.9.X LONG TERM STABLE version is now at end of life for bug fixing/support since December 2025.
   A new LTS (based on the current 3.5.X) is starting now and will act as the stable release for the coming years.

0.4.0 (XX/XX/XX):
   HS: MadLoop computes the one-loop QCD virtual of processes with S-wave bound states
       (quarkonia, leptonia; colour singlets and octets of any J) in the final state with
       elementary particles, e.g. "generate g g > Jpsi(1|3S11) g [virt=QCD]" with the new
       model loop_sm_onia (loop_sm with the Fock states of sm_onia; tools/make_onia_model.py
       adds them to any loop UFO model). The constituent loop amplitudes are projected
       through an effective Born per constituent helicity; the threshold integrals are
       reduced by TIRust (default reduction chain 9|3 for these outputs); the IR poles are
       predicted from colour-linked projected Borns. 't Hooft-Veltman scheme, Coulomb term
       dropped at zero relative velocity. Processes without a Born and FKS modes are
       refused for now. See docs/onia_loop.md and docs/benchmarkfolder.
   HS: MadLoop: one P-wave bound state per process at one loop ("generate g g >
       chic0(1|3P01) [virt=QCD]"). The derivative in the relative momentum is computed in
       dual numbers: dual HELAS and loop vertex routines (ALOHA dual mode, now also for the
       L routines), the loop polynomials with their derivative, and the propagator
       derivatives as tensor integrals with one doubled propagator reduced by TIRust
       (raised propagator powers, new in vendor/tirust: tirust_tensor_evaluate_powers).
       Double and quadruple precision (MP_Dual numbers, aloha mp_dual_variables.f and
       aloha_functions_mp_dual.f; the QP rescue of MadLoop's stability tests). The
       Coulomb box with a doubled gluon is not yet reduced by TIRust at threshold: such a
       point is reported unstable in every CTModeRun mode. The pole prediction includes
       the P -> S pole of the soft current (arXiv:2402.19221, appendix B).
   HS: MadLoop: outputs with bound states add to the double-precision accuracy an estimate
       of the rounding of the counterterm interference, epsilon sqrt(sum t^2)/|finite|
       over its terms, which the stability test cannot measure (it does not vary the
       counterterms). Near a soft jet the heavy-quark R2 terms carry the mass squared and
       cancel in the projected helicity sum. Such points are rescued in quadruple
       precision: g g > Jpsi g with a 1.5 MeV gluon was 22% off (1e4 GeV b, t) or 1.7e-4
       off (physical masses) with an accuracy of 1e-9.
   HS: MadLoop: one-loop QCD virtual of the decay of an S- or P-wave bound state, colour
       singlet or octet ('generate etac(1|1S01) > a a [virt=QCD]'), on the tree level of the onium
       decays (incoming projector, decay LDME of Block ldme_decay). It reproduces the
       textbook corrections of eta_c, chi_c0,2 > a a and J/psi > e+ e-, and the
       H > l+ l- a widths of He and Huang (arXiv:2608.23439). A P-wave state exactly at rest
       decaying into three particles is reported unstable (TIRust): evaluate the summed
       width in a boosted frame.
   HS: MadLoop: ONIA_SELECT_JZ(K,NJZ,JZS) restricts a bound state to chosen J_z (its
       helicity in the frame of the momenta): polarised one-loop virtuals, e.g. the
       transverse and longitudinal J/psi of g g > Jpsi g.
   HS: MadLoop standalone outputs compile their sources at -O2 (MADLOOP_OPTIMIZATION in the
       loop makefile, before FFLAGS) and their DHELAS and MODEL libraries at -O2 (ALOHA_FLAG
       and the new MODEL_FLAG of make_opts); they tabulate the colour factors and
       split-order indices of the counterterm and loop-coefficient contractions at
       initialisation, and check the IEEE modes of the private DP-terminal updaters once
       per coefficient batch instead of in every call (the DP-terminal proof certifies the
       new caller statements and admits exactly these flags): identical results, 17-35%
       less time per point on the benchmark processes.
   OM: madevent rejected every phase-space point with a dynamical factorisation scale below
       2 GeV whenever lpp was not 0, a floor that only makes sense for hadron PDFs: it
       silently cut the threshold region, e.g. 27% of PbPb > a a > ta+ ta- at 5.52 TeV
       without cuts (846 instead of 1163 mub). Each beam now has the lower limit of its own
       density: 2 GeV for hadron PDFs (|lpp|=1), 1 GeV for the tabulated dressed-lepton
       densities (the lower edge of their grids), none for the gamma-UPC fluxes, which do not
       depend on the scale, the analytic isrbet* sets, the EPA (iww) and EVA, which handle low
       scales themselves. A dynamical renormalisation scale is now raised to at least 2 GeV,
       as at NLO, so that alpha_s is not evaluated at its Landau pole (it gave alpha_s = 9e98
       for e.g. a a > mu+ mu- without cuts once the factorisation-scale cut was removed).
   HS: Bug fix (gamma-UPC): in collisions of two different nuclei (A /= B), the Woods-Saxon
       thickness of beam B (TAhat_WoodsSaxon with IMETH=2) was interpolated on the grid of beam
       A, which made the probability of no hadronic interaction, and so the photon-photon
       luminosity, wrong (EDFF and ChFF alike). Identical beams (PbPb, OO, ...) are not affected.
   HS+OM: Bug fix (gamma-UPC): in pA collisions with the ChFF flux and the nucleus as beam 1
       (e.g. Pb+p), the photon number densities of the proton and of the nucleus were evaluated
       with the kinematics of each other, so the photon-photon luminosity of A+p differed from
       the one of p+A. p+A and the EDFF flux are not affected.
   OM: gamma-UPC photon fluxes (pdlabel ed*/ch* with lpp 2, proton and ion beams, neutron
       tagging) in mg7/madspace. madevent's own gamma-UPC luminosity is tabulated once on a
       grid of both photon momentum fractions (it does not factorise) and interpolated by
       madspace, so it runs on any device and travels with gridpacks. The table is cached in
       Source/PDF/gammaUPC/tables (about 10 s for protons, 5 min for Pb). The phase space is
       generated per nucleon (e_cm = sqrt(s_NN)); the cross sections are those of the ion-ion
       collision, as in madevent, and the LHE beams are the ions. The interpolation follows
       the luminosity to ~1e-6 for protons; for ions to ~1e-5 up to W_gamma-gamma ~ 100 GeV
       and to 0.1-1% for 200-500 GeV. It reproduces the MG7 paper LO results for
       PbPb > a a > ta+ ta- in all neutron categories.
   HS+OM: madevent: lpp = 2 with a pdlabel that is neither gamma-UPC, iww nor none now falls
       back to chff (was edff), the default of mg7 too.
   RF: "set low_mem_multicore_nlo_generation True" (NLO generation and output on nb_core cores,
       the matrix elements kept in temporary files) gives again the output of the serial mode.
       It differed for instance in the virtuals (nlo_mixed_expansion False, explicit orders,
       a --loop_filter given to one 'add process' only, which it applied to all of them), the
       reals after an 'add process' (also those of another NLO mode or diagram filter: of
       g g > t t~ [QCD] for u u~ > t t~ [real=QCD]), initial_states_map.dat (empty for
       [LOonly]), the order of the directories, proc_characteristics and the default
       MadAnalysis5 card (not tailored to the processes). In this mode too, a process without
       any correction up to NLO is refused when it is generated (it gave an output without
       corrections), and the matrix elements are generated before an existing output
       directory is replaced. An 'add process' after changing the option (which lost virtuals
       or reals) is now refused: all the processes of an output are generated in one mode.
       A process of this mode which dies (e.g. out of memory) now stops the output with an
       error instead of making it wait for ever, and a failed fork no longer leaves ctrl+c
       ignored for the rest of the session.
       Also in the serial mode: after an 'add process', a born could get the real matrix
       element of another process definition with other orders (g a > t t~ [QCD QED] that of
       g g > t t~ [QCD QED]), and through it the MC histories of the other definition; an
       output after an earlier output and an 'add process' lacked the added processes; a
       process with --ewsudakov added to an identical one without (u u~ > z z [LOonly=QCD],
       then c c~ > z z [LOonly=QCD] --ewsudakov) was combined with it, losing its Sudakov.
   RF: NLO output: the born-to-real configuration map of real_from_born_configs.inc, from
       which the H events take the tree of their event record (its s-channel resonances), was
       only written for the P directories with only QCD splittings, and depended on the
       directories written before (through the splitting types accumulated in
       proc_characteristic): with --allow_qed_ps, the H events of all the other directories
       read an undefined configuration (also for their QCD splittings). Each FKS
       configuration now gets its map, QED ones too, whatever the other ones and the other
       directories. A born configuration gets the real configuration of its diagram with the
       splitting; when the orders of the real amplitude exclude that diagram (mixed QCD and
       QED orders, e.g. the s-channel photon and Z of u u~ > t t~ [QCD QED] for a photon
       emission), the first real configuration of its topology, else (a topology of the EW
       diagrams only, e.g. the t-channel W of b b~ > t t~) the first one with the splitting.
       An [LOonly] directory always gets its trivial map. Outputs without QED splittings are
       unchanged, apart from the [LOonly] directories (no run reads their map). Also, the
       special case of the 2 -> 1 borns of this mapping always failed with python3.
   RF: standalone FKS output (output --fks): HTML/info.html listed only the last P directory
       (made before the others were stripped down to the Born check); it lists all of them.
   RF: NLO output from a script or from Python (not from the prompt, which imports sm when it
       starts) whose first generate had to import the model itself wrote no
       SubProcesses/procdef_mg5.dat, so that index.html stayed the "under preparation" page,
       also after each launch.
   OM: color_basis ddm (and auto, for the outputs where auto picks it): the (n-2)!
       Del Duca-Dixon-Maltoni colour basis is now also used when the gluons come with
       colour singlets, e.g. g g > h + n g in the Higgs effective theory (any number of
       scalars or pseudoscalars). The adjoint delta of the g g H vertex made these
       processes fall back silently on the (n-1)! trace basis (120 instead of 24 colour
       structures for g g > h + 4g). With 'set color_basis ddm' the processes that keep
       the trace basis, and why, are now listed at info level. An explicit 'set
       color_basis ddm' now only applies to the outputs which can use that basis: the
       others keep the trace basis and say so (the Matchbox outputs, which pass its
       color strings on; mg7_v5, which crashed on it; the loop and NLO outputs), and
       'check' no longer leaves it on for the next command.
   OM: 'output pythia8' (dropped in MadGraph7) is now refused by the NLO interface too,
       where 'pythia8' was taken as the name of the output directory, and the message
       says how to use Pythia 8 instead: shower the events of a madevent, mg7 or NLO
       output (shower=Pythia8 at the launch question). The help no longer advertises
       it, and 'output <format> auto' no longer writes into the Pythia 8 directory for
       the formats without an automatic directory name (it used pythia8_path): it now
       writes into a new PROC_<format>_<i> directory (e.g. 'output matrix auto' gives
       PROC_matrix_0), which replaces the Pythia8_proc_<i> one. The code that wrote
       Pythia 8 process and model code is removed (the Pythia 8 exporters of
       export_cpp.py, template_files/pythia8, the unused '-name' output option, and
       'launch' on a Pythia 8 directory, which now says that this is no longer
       supported, from the LO, MadLoop and aMC@NLO interfaces alike).
   OM: standalone/mg7 with --mask=False (no flavour guard) gave wrong |M|^2 for some flavours
       of merged electroweak processes (p p > w+ w- j j QCD=0: 24 of 54, d d~ > w+ w- u u~
       x55). This was also the root cause of the --hel_recycling bug of 0.3.0: the C++ flavoured
       routines accepted a fermion pair in both orientations of its coupling table, which for
       a W coupling is the W of the opposite charge. They now read it in one orientation, as
       the Fortran routines do (Fortran --mask=False was right). Also:
       - an output whose --mask differs from an earlier output of the same processes, or
         that follows an add process, no longer reuses that output's matrix elements and
         trimmed diagrams;
       - a subprocess of more than 64 flavour groups (p p > j j j j QCD=0) keeps its flavour
         guards;
       - every output (Fortran and C++) refuses a flavoured coupling its flavour tables
         cannot hold: an unmerged fermion coupled, as the first fermion of the vertex, to
         several flavours of a merged one (e.g. a heavy quark xq mixing into d and s; none
         in the bundled models). The tables kept one of them without a word: the Fortran
         standalone gave |M|^2 = 0 for g d~ > xq~ z. Use "set apply_flavor_grouping False"
         and explicit flavours for such a model;
       - "save processes" / "load processes" are removed (the amplitudes kept after an
         output have lost the diagrams its --mask trimmed). "save model", "load model" and
         "save options" remain.
   OM: flavour grouping: a process in which a merged quark is built from unmerged particles
       only, through a flavoured coupling with several flavours (a heavy quark xq mixing into
       d and s through the Z: p p > xq z j, xq xq~ > z z; none in the bundled models), crashed
       the output with a FlavorTagError and a "report this bug" message (xq xq~ > z z, with no
       merged external leg, only while writing check_sa.f). The merged output gives each
       wavefunction the one flavour its inputs fix: it can neither take this one from the
       other end of the fermion line nor sum over it. The output now stops before writing any
       subprocess, with an error naming the process and the couplings; generate such a
       process after "set apply_flavor_grouping False" and a new import of the model.
   OM: polarised propagators of a massive vector (w+{0}, w+{T}, w+{A}, w+{S}, ... on a
       particle decayed further) in the FD gauge: {0}, {T}, {+}, {-}, {A}, {S}, {0S} are the
       pieces of the five-component FD propagator (the FD longitudinal vector of the
       external W, the unitary transverse ones, and (q^2-M^2) n n/(n.q)^2). The FD output
       used to be wrong (SIGBUS in the Fortran standalone). {T}, {+}, {-} equal the
       unitary-gauge ones, {0} equals it on shell; {A}, {S}, {0S} are the FD remainder
       (a warning says so). A line at rest has its helicity axis along +z, as in the
       unitary gauge. A massless vector keeps only {T}, {+}, {-} in the FD gauge.
   OM: polarised propagators of a massive vector are refused in the Feynman gauge (the
       output crashed), in the complex-mass scheme, and {G}, {H}, {Q}, {W} in the FD gauge
       (also by ALOHA itself).
   OM: madmatrix: a custom propagator denominator with a width term (the polarised
       propagators) was written as a Python complex literal and did not compile.
   OM: madmatrix, FD gauge, flavour grouping on (the default): |M|^2 was 0 for a W/Goldstone
       line between merged and unmerged flavours (t > b ta+ vt, ta+ vt > t b~): the
       combined vector + Goldstone routine assumed a flavour-diagonal coupling.
   OM: check gauge accepts a decay chain, e.g. 'check gauge t > w+{0} b, w+ > ta+ vt' (the
       other checks still refuse them). The chain is compared in the unitary, axial and FD
       gauges (not Feynman, where it has no Goldstone boson), with every decayed particle on
       shell and a width of 1e-9 M: the helicity pieces agree to 1e-12, {A}, {S} and {0S}
       are gauge dependent. Refused in the complex-mass scheme and with loops.
   OM: check gauge/brs/lorentz/permutation/full with flavour grouping (the default):
       the flavour of the legs was dropped and every grouped process was evaluated for
       the first flavour of each group only. A charged-current process such as
       `u d~ > w+ g` (evaluated as d d~ > w+ g, which vanishes) was checked by nothing:
       an empty gauge table ("Summary: 0/0 passed"), no Ward check, and a "Passed"
       0 = 0 permutation check. The checks now evaluate the flavour asked for, and a
       multiparticle such as `p p > w+ w-` each physical flavour, as without grouping.
       A check that could check no process now says so instead of printing an empty
       table.
   OM: the FD gauge (Feynman-diagram gauge, arXiv:2203.10440, 2405.01256) is now chosen
       automatically for each process line whose diagrams have a W/Z propagator ending on a
       vertex that is not a conserved current (HVV, VVV, VVVV, or a massive fermion such as t-b):
       VBF/VBS, u u > h j j j j, p p > w+ w-, t t~ with leptonic W decays switch; Drell-Yan,
       u d~ > e+ ve g, p p > z z and pure QCD do not. In the unitary gauge the diagrams of such a
       process grow with the energy and digits are lost in their sum, in the FD gauge the
       cancellation happens diagram by diagram. When every line needs it the whole output is in
       the FD gauge ('set gauge FD' in the proc_card), otherwise only those lines are
       ('--gauge=FD' on them). 'set gauge X' always wins, 'set gauge default' hands the choice
       back, 'set auto_gauge False' turns it off. Never for loop processes, spin above one,
       bound states, [amplicol] processes, polarised legs, propagator polarisations, or models
       without (checked) goldstone bosons. The FD-gauge citation is triggered, the mg7 output
       records the gauge in its proc_characteristics. Costs 6-27% per phase-space point, with
       an unweighting efficiency equal or better.
   OM: new process option 'generate|add process ... --gauge=FD|unitary': the outputs madevent,
       standalone_fortran, standalone and mg7 hold subprocesses in both gauges (Fortran:
       Source/DHELAS_FD, C++: src/HelAmps_<model>_fd.h), the other formats refuse it. MadSpin
       and the reweighting read such a proc_card in one gauge (the reweighting: FD when every
       process can be, else unitary). Not available after 'add model' or 'customize_model'.
   OM: FD gauge in the complex-mass scheme: the goldstone part of the propagator used the
       conjugate mass. Outputs in one gauge are otherwise unchanged. The multiparticles of the
       user survive the reload of the model behind 'set gauge'. While an 'output
       --postpone_model' waits for its model, the gauge of the processes written in it is kept.
   OM: complex-mass scheme: check gauge no longer stops at its axial step (a custom
       denominator is written unless it uses the width); madmatrix (standalone, mg7)
       compiles and agrees with Fortran; MadSpin uses madmatrix density matrices; the $
       exclusion window works (it was silently wrong in Fortran, did not compile in C++);
       $ on a massless particle is refused. check language evaluates every backend at the
       same phase-space point, and the C++ FD vxxxxx has the FD longitudinal polarisation.

0.3.0 (XX/XX/XX):
   OM: --hel_recycling (standalone/mg7, CPU/SIMD builds, on by default): the recycled matrix
       element left out the flavour masks of a flavour-merged subprocess, on the assumption
       that an amplitude masked for a flavour vanishes there anyway. It does not always (a
       diagram closed by a bosonic vertex, fed by fermion currents that follow the coupling
       partner tables): |M|^2 was wrong for some flavours of merged electroweak multi-quark
       processes with charged currents. p p > w+ w- j j QCD=0: 12 of 36 flavours, e.g.
       d d~ > w+ w- u u~ x55, d u~ > w+ w- s c~ x169, and 83.7 pb instead of 0.934 pb in a
       MadSpace run. Every copy of such an amplitude is now guarded with its mask, as in the
       plain build. GPU builds, --hel_recycling=False and MadEvent were not affected; outputs
       made before this fix must be regenerated. Also: a recycled build cut into chunks
       (HelRecyclingChunks_*.cc) did not compile (ndcoup undeclared).
   OM: --hel_recycling (standalone/mg7): the good helicities and the amplitudes that
       vanish, which the recycled matrix element is built for, are now measured again at
       the param_card a run is launched with (after the launch question, and at each point
       of a scan), and at the cards of a MadtRex parameter reweighting. They were only
       measured at generation, at the output's default card: a coupling set to zero at
       launch was still evaluated, and an amplitude that vanishes at the default card but
       not at the launch card was silently dropped. The probe build is kept in .hrprobe,
       so that a new card costs a run of it (milliseconds); a card at which the filter
       comes out the same rewrites and recompiles nothing, and a build for another filter
       than the output's has its own library name (lib..._P*_hr<hash>_*.so).
   OM: reweighting applies the beam polarisation of the run_card (polbeam1/2) (#394).
   OM: madevent/MadSpin: polarised beams with a mirror process gave beam 1's polarisation
       to the particle of beam 2 (#394).
   RF: Preserve the legacy scalar-scale fallback for MC@NLO-Delta events
       with an unspecified mur when reading MadGraph7 scale metadata.
   RF: Remove UNLOPS merging and NNLL+NLO jet-veto resummation, including their
       dedicated cuts, clustering, reweighting utilities, PDF grids and LHE
       records. NLO runs now accept only ickkw=0 (no merging) and ickkw=3 (FxFx);
       cards selecting removed modes are rejected.
   RF: Refactor MC@NLO subtraction around native labelled matching histories and
       an output-wide BornSupport library. Equivalent Born evaluators share
       amplitudes, helicity sums and colour/spin correlations, with explicit
       transfers of model parameters, momenta and subprocess metadata. Native
       histories retain their own Born projections, PDFs, damping and weights.
       The FKS_params.dat controls MCExplicitKLSum, FKSExplicitSum and
       MCSubtractionAtFixedFlow select complete inner matching-history sums,
       outer FKS-sector sums and subtraction at the sampled event colour flow.
   RF: Split FKS phase-space generation into typed Born/real/counterevent state,
       sampling and forward/inverse radiation maps. Native Born projections,
       subtraction, recoil and event writing use the same kinematic state.
       Resonance channels use local recoil with analytic maps and Jacobians.
       FKSFinalRecoil=1 or 2 selects a massless incoming parton with a hadronic
       PDF as recoiler at fixed order; this option is unavailable for MC@NLO.
   RF: Compute MC@NLO-Delta shower scales in native Fortran, removing the
       Pythia-backed runtime dependency of the matching integrator. Update
       shower-scale, event-colour and Sudakov bookkeeping, including massive
       radiators, Pythia 8.318/FxFx patches and Herwig7 integration, configured
       through herwig7_path.
   RF: Add optional born_spreading in the NLO run_card, fitting a normalized
       Born radiation distribution to reduce negative S-event weights. Fits
       are validated before use and saved per FKS sector. Add the fitted
       virtual surrogate controlled by VirtTrickSurrogate: 0 retains the
       grid/polynomial, 1 uses colour dipoles and 2 also uses Born-pair logs.
       Repeat the initial channel scan after bad-iteration recovery resets
       the grids, including when a Born-spreading fit is already ready;
       calibrated restarts retain their adapted channel weights.
   RF: Preserve MadGraph7 polarisation and me_frame choices through native Born
       and real evaluation. Born helicity masks follow the model, context and
       requested frame; shared real caches restore diagram weights as well as
       split-order results. Mixed QCD/QED Born amplitudes retain their separate
       strong-coupling powers when cached amplitudes are rescaled.
       Model snapshots now contain only used point-dependent parameters and
       numerical couplings, selected using the model converter's dependency
       metadata and reads in the generated evaluators. Used fixed inputs are
       installed once per model configuration; card/coupling reinitialization
       refreshes that configuration and invalidates dependent caches. Running
       external parameters are included, and unused model inputs are omitted.
   RF: Propagate MINT integration uncertainties with the actual iteration
       weights, including individual channels and the last-three-iteration
       estimate. Retain the existing central-value and histogram weights;
       accuracy-driven runs can need more iterations with the corrected error.
       Handle zero signed estimates and zero-error channels in these updates.
   RF: Use scalar virtual fits only with one Born normalizer matching the fit.
       Other order combinations retain the per-order grid/polynomial, avoiding
       excessive virtual sampling with mixed QCD/EW corrections. Protect the
       virtual-to-Born diagnostic when the Born contribution is zero.
   RF: Add massive/QED subtraction and shower-support checks. Experimental NLO
       QED shower launches require --allow_qed_ps; QED Delta/FxFx matching and
       shower channels excluded by the support checks remain unsupported.
       PYTHIA6PT final-state radiation is rejected before compilation, using

       their existing availability.
   RF: Regenerate existing NLO process outputs for the new BornSupport library,

   RF: Clean up aborted local-worker batches after their workers stop,

   RF: Transport the transverse spin four-vector to the Born frame for

   RF: Stabilize massive final-state radiation bounds and both solutions

   RF: Use the current emitter mass for the PYTHIA6Q starting-scale bound,

   OM: output --axial_gauge=True (madevent, standalone_fortran, standalone, mg7) now also
       chooses the spin axis of the massive final-state fermions when at least two of the
       massless vectors they couple to share a reference: their spin is quantised along that
       reference momentum instead of along their own momentum (new HELAS
       routines IXXXXXR/OXXXXXR). More helicity configurations and amplitudes then vanish and

       massless vector or none, for a polarised process, for an

   OM: mg7 (madspace) now supports the run_card "me_frame", with the same meaning
       as in madevent: it names the external particles whose momentum sum is put
       at rest, and the matrix element is evaluated on momenta boosted into that
       frame. Only the matrix element sees them -- the cuts, the scales, the
       momentum fractions and the written event all keep the momenta as
       generated. It only changes the result for a matrix element that is not
       Lorentz invariant, i.e. a polarised one, and it is then the frame the
       polarisations are defined in; the default [] applies no boost, so every

   OM: BUG FIX (mg7): the initial-state mirror paired each event with a matrix
       element evaluated on the momenta *before* the mirror was applied. madspace

       the event by pi about x (py, pz -> -py, -pz), but only the written event

       because |M|^2 is invariant under that rotation -- which a polarised matrix
       element is not once it is evaluated in a frame that holds the polarised

       is affected (checked: p p > z j unchanged at 12420 +- 69 pb). The matrix
       element is now evaluated on the momenta the event is written with, which
       is both what madevent does and what the systematics reweighting here
       already assumed, since it reads them back out of the event buffer.
