Local direct-R2 feasibility boundary¶

Historical: this describes the per-diagram legacy one-loop engine, removed on 2026-10-29 (MQ-LEGACY-REMOVE; v2 is the only one-loop engine, see loop_v2/DESIGN.md and §13.3 there). The modules, tools and tests named here may no longer exist.

tools/loop_reference/local_r2_oracle.py is an executable local-kernel oracle. It does not enable the public direct-R2 strategy, certify a complete physical process, or add loop-induced observables. The bounded physical gluon control below separately exercises complete model and direct strategies. Public direct dispatch remains disabled. A direct selection must cover every contributing graph; these experiments do not justify mixing direct and model completion graph by graph.

Reused local state¶

CoefficientDAG now optionally accepts extra polynomial variables and explicit nonnegative rank weights. The ordinary four-momentum interface is unchanged. The oracle uses (q0, q1, q2, q3, mu, epsilon) with weights (1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 0). Each sparse coefficient retains its tensor component and all six powers. mu is a slash parameter, not the reducer’s mu2: only after spin closure are even powers mapped to mu2**(power/2).

Every transition contracts one dependency tensor with a placeholder coefficient current. Symbolica extracts coefficients and saves scalar evaluator instructions. The existing Rust CoefficientPlan executes those instructions over native binary64, DoubleFloat or arbitrary-precision buffers. No current is expanded into a whole-diagram expression in that numerical path. Exact expansion occurs only in the small independent comparison oracle. Weighted rank is structural; relations between predecessor coefficients can lower the actual polynomial rank.

Closed fermion-chain experiment¶

The tested chain is the stripped massive scalar–fermion–two-vector triangle in both ordered orientations, with physical vector ports and one common evanescent loop momentum. Its local propagator is represented by the dependency’s explicit four-dimensional matrices as

slash(q4 + shift) + m + i*mu*gamma5.

Here gamma5 is an auxiliary matrix realization, not a physical axial coupling or a D-dimensional gamma-five prescription. Dependency contractions verify its square -mu**2, anticommutation with each physical gamma, the massive propagator product, trace(1)=4, and unchanged evanescent part under a physical routing shift. No matrix entries or Clifford simplifier are implemented here.

Odd-mu coefficients are retained through every open segment. At closure the D-dimensional trace selects even evanescent parity. The component realization otherwise produces nonzero spurious odd traces. A negative control that drops odd currents early loses the required even rational numerator. This prescription is checked against an independent Idenso D-dimensional trace for all sixteen vector tensor components, both orientations, arbitrary symbolic fermion mass, and two routings, including a nontrivial rational four-dimensional shift. The independent trace uses Pi_D.Pj_D = Pi_4.Pj_4 - mu**2 for all routed loop momenta, with physical external ports. No rotational integration identity is used pointwise.

The physical transverse projection, with the normalization of tools/loop_reference/diphoton_r2.json, yields y*mu2 per orientation. Native coefficient buffers from both orientations are combined before NinjaRS reduction. The massive subthreshold triangle yields finite y, with both poles zero. Changing/empty batches and original lane identities are checked in all three arithmetic modes. Inputs beyond binary64 resolution remain visible in both DoubleFloat and arbitrary precision. This local experiment agrees with the existing separately generated full-diagram diphoton oracle; it does not import arbitrary UFO diagrams into direct kernels.

A second local transition uses Idenso to reduce the open word gamma_a gamma_b gamma^a before materializing its remaining physical port. The exact factor (2-D) survives; closure against a physical temporal gamma produces -8+8*epsilon at D=4-2*epsilon. Its native reduction against the massless spacelike bubble checks that the epsilon times single pole contributes +8 to the finite coefficient. This certifies that particular contracted kernel and the epsilon transport/convolution, not a universal vector-loop rule set.

Dependency audit and remaining gates¶

The inspected Rust snapshot is the existing pinned FeynKit 474478a7a80bc9f9f21bead0e1cde6acbcffbe93. Executable Python probes use the installed Symbolica Community distribution 3.0.2, whose kernel reports 3.0.1. These are the current development dependency selections; the existing source publication/package-coherence gate is unchanged.

The three checks were source, public API/documentation, and regression/examples:

  • Source: crates/idenso/src/dirac/simplify.rs, DiracRuleDimension::{gamma_compatible,merge_known_gamma_dimension}, requires matching known Lorentz dimensions for ordinary dimensional identities and explicit dimension four for four-dimensional identities. This does not encode orthogonal physical and evanescent Clifford subspaces.

  • Public API: crates/spynso3/src/expression.rs, dirac_gamma, gamma5, with_lorentz_dimension, simplify_algebra, and undo_chain; the corresponding installed symbolica.community.tensor stubs document four-dimensional explicit matrices. with_lorentz_dimension promotes four-dimensional ports; it is not a split-space constructor. Its scalar coefficients are deliberately unchanged.

  • Tests/examples: crates/idenso/src/dirac/test/fixed_frame_regressions.rs preserves symbolic (D-4) spectator factors. Community’s examples/hep/gamma_simplification.py explicitly keeps mixed dimensions and D-dimensional gamma-five words unreduced. Its Higgs diphoton example/test establishes a full-trace evanescent oracle, not local split-space transitions.

No dedicated general 4 + (D-4) Clifford transition/closure API was found in those surfaces or the relevant tensor/idenso source searches. Existing feynkit-tensor::TensorReducer supplies integrated tensor projection; it is not an evanescent local spin-state representation and is not substituted for NinjaRS. The restricted prototype therefore reuses existing supported matrices, traces, polynomial operations and evaluators; it introduces no replacement algebra.

Before production direct completion, the model compiler must certify source fermion templates, signs, gauge, dimensional spin conventions and complete graph coverage. Open fermion chains, physical chiral/projector vertices, arbitrary D-vector/ghost contractions, multiple independent evanescent structures and anomaly-sensitive traces remain outside this certificate. There is no universal FDH-to-HV correction inferred from these results. General split-space support, if required for those domains, is an explicit dependency/API design gate rather than a private algebra implementation. No dependency was modified by this work.

Graph-derived closed vectorlike cycles¶

ClosedVectorlikeFermionKernels now plugs into the ordinary dependency-owned open-loop traversal. Before materialization it verifies each cycle propagator against its source normalization*(slash(P)+m) and each cycle vertex against a scalar identity or one physical vector gamma. Directed opposite fermion flows, unit loop signatures and the common sign of the native affine routing are checked explicitly. The extra slash carries both the source momentum signature and the routing sign. Physical momentum variables remain separate from auxiliary polynomial variables when constructing exact denominators and S matrices.

Exact checks use actual massive-quark two-, three- and four-gluon cycles from the QCD model’s four-gluon graph set, including their off-shell tree attachments, both oriented words, alternative bubble cuts and canonical family routing. An independent oracle contracts the unmodified source graph in D dimensions before selecting physical external ports. All six even closed polynomials agree; the box contains nonzero mu-four terms. Local odd factors are checked before closure, since the complete trace can already be even and hide a wrong common routing sign. The construction accepts longer certified chains within the reducer’s domain; this graph-derived test set currently covers lengths two to four.

The bubble and box are also reduced through the native local coefficient DAG and NinjaRS in double, DoubleFloat and 80-digit arbitrary arithmetic. An independently compiled full-D-trace polynomial supplies the coefficient oracle; both feed the same reducer, so this checks the graph-to-reducer boundary rather than supplying an independent master-integral oracle. Real and complex fermion masses, canonical family ordering, exact S matrices, original lane IDs and batch sizes 1, 5, 0, 33, 1 are covered. All pole coefficients vanish for these positive-mu rational numerators and their nonzero finite parts agree. These checks use NinjaRS 5f35b07 and oneloopmaster 264a6ce; the separate analytic scalar anchors and physical Born-interference controls retain their own validation responsibilities.

This provider remains an internal graph-numerator prototype. It does not certify physical axial vertices, open fermion chains, internal D-vector/ghost states or whole-process direct completion, and does not mix direct and effective-rule completion within a process. Tree attachments retain the supported physical four-dimensional source rules. Source-template checks do not by themselves prove all possible model conventions or gauge sectors.

Massless Yang–Mills and ghost state experiment¶

DimensionalYangMillsKernels supplies a second bounded graph provider. It continues the source covariant metric propagators and momentum/metric cubic and quartic vertices using dependency tensors. The accepted graph has one pure massless vector or ghost cycle and physical massless-vector tree attachments. Longitudinal propagator additions, higher-derivative vertices, massive particles, mixed spin cycles and unexpected source ports are rejected. These are algebraic source-template checks, not a claim of gauge invariance for an arbitrary model.

The local state has six explicit coordinates: four physical directions, the common extra loop-momentum direction, and one transverse spectator. Every source momentum has fifth component a_i * routing_sign * mu and zero sixth component; physical external states and tree momenta have both extra components zero. The source rule itself is promoted with with_lorentz_dimension(6), so all physical/extra transitions and their metric signs come from the dependency. Odd-mu transitions survive every open current. A negative control that projects each local rule to even mu loses nonzero closed even terms.

The native rank-one spectator projector is normalized by its own Minkowski norm. Exactly one chosen cycle propagator receives (D-6)*P_s, at D=4-2*epsilon. Its six-state metric trace is therefore D, and the spectator weight is D-5 = -1-2*epsilon. Dependency component contractions show that physical/aligned states do not mix with the spectator, that distinct spectator states do not mix, and that their diagonal transitions agree. These checks include arbitrary symbolic physical attachments and each local colour term of an actual quartic QCD source vertex; they compare six and seven component libraries. The accepted metric/momentum templates have no further transverse structure. Changing the weighted edge and the cut independently preserves the closed numerator. Independently generated unweighted five- and six-dimensional recurrences also satisfy N(D) = N5 + (D-5)*(N6-N5) on the tested graphs.

Actual vector bubble/triangle and ghost bubble/triangle graphs with off-shell tree attachments agree exactly with an independent raw-source D-dimensional contraction before physical materialization. The vector results retain nonzero mu-dependent and explicit epsilon terms. The ghost cycles retain the native source statistics/global factors and agree with their four-dimensional numerators: a physical attachment has no extra component for its ghost momentum vertex to contract. This does not omit their ordinary four-dimensional bare contribution or its denominator-induced rational terms.

For this domain, the epsilon-free, mu-free closure equals the original four-dimensional source numerator. rational_sector_columns selects the remaining closed even-mu and epsilon columns. The vector bubble and triangle then pass through the native coefficient scheduler, exact S-matrix binding, NinjaRS numerator sectors and Laurent convolution in double, DoubleFloat and 80-digit arbitrary arithmetic. An independently compiled full-D polynomial provides the coefficient oracle; both use the same reduction library. Native batch sizes 1, 5, 0, 33, 1 preserve row identity, and coupling differences below binary64 resolution remain visible in the reduced result. All three Laurent coefficients are compared. These checks do not introduce a second reducer or a process-level model-R2 comparison.

The dependency inventory for this construction was checked at three surfaces:

  • Source: crates/spynso3/src/structure.rs exposes Representation::mink with signature (+,-,...,-); expression.rs::with_lorentz_dimension promotes source index spaces before contraction. crates/spenso/src/network/library/ supplies dimension-dependent metrics and native tensor materialization.

  • Public Python API/documentation: Representation.mink, TensorExpression.with_lorentz_dimension, TensorLibrary signatures, Tensor.dense, Tensor.dot and component access support the entire local construction. The projector’s norm and all contractions use these APIs.

  • Existing regressions/examples: fixed_basis_products_reject_unproven_or_incompatible_components and the adjacent larger-space metric checks in crates/spenso/src/network/library/symbolic.rs, plus crates/feynkit-py/tests/installed_metric_factory.py and installed_dimensional_spin_sums.py, establish arbitrary-dimension metric behavior and symbolic-dimensional traces. No dedicated physical/evanescent local gauge-cycle constructor was found in these source/API/example searches; the prototype composes the existing metric, projector and routing operations.

These first integrated graph checks cover cubic vector/ghost bubbles and triangles; the complete retained gluon-graph coverage below extends them. Massive gauge and Goldstone sectors, physical chiral vertices, open fermion chains and mixed spin cycles remain outside this provider. No universal scheme-conversion formula is inferred, and no dependency source was modified.

Complete retained gluon-graph kernel coverage¶

direct_kernels.dimensional_kernels(graph) selects the closed vectorlike fermion or massless Yang–Mills/ghost provider. Binding still checks every local source factor. Unsupported or mixed cycles fail explicitly; this dispatch does not establish eligibility for a complete process.

Every retained graph builds with the selected dimensional provider. The largest structural weighted numerator rank is four. No tadpole remains in this source selection; this observation does not certify tadpoles in other processes. At an exact physical momentum control with general external vector components, all auxiliary-free polynomial coefficients agree exactly with ordinary four-dimensional recurrences, retaining each separate native colour tensor. The comparison covers all 186 graphs, including both fermion orientations and massless and massive source quarks. It is a full coefficient comparison at that kinematic control, not a proof over all possible phase space.

Independent full-D source contractions additionally cover a one-quartic bubble, a two-quartic bubble, a quartic triangle, a vector box and a ghost box. Each uses two nonzero exact external-colour projections. This small oracle expands and contracts the original unsplit source graph with dependency operations; it does not substitute recurrence instructions. Both the dimensional trace and the local recurrence retain the entire loop-momentum polynomial. The two-quartic bubble is also invariant under independent choices of cut and weighted edge, so its dimensional multiplicity enters exactly once.

closed_reduction_powers(recurrence, node=...) is the shared reducer boundary. It maps scalar closed output labels to (q0,q1,q2,q3,mu2,epsilon) powers and allows scalar descendants such as a tree-denominator multiplication. For a closed vectorlike fermion, None explicitly denotes the proven odd auxiliary gamma5-trace projection; such nonzero auxiliary terms are expected and must not be rejected as if they were physical evanescent traces. No open current may be projected. Gauge/ghost closures must instead have no odd-mu labels and reject them. Fermion epsilon powers and more than one gauge dimensional-trace factor also reject. The auxiliary-free columns give N4; the remaining accepted columns give the direct dimensional remainder before reduction and Laurent convolution.

This kernel coverage does not itself enable public direct generation. Complete Born interference, model-R2 agreement in the same HV convention, exclusive process-wide completion, precision and performance remain responsibilities of the physical assembler and its independent controls.

Complete physical gluon diagnostic¶

gluon_scattering_model_virtual_program(..., r2_strategy="direct") is an internal full-SU(3) control in the same HV convention as its source-model path. Every selected loop uses its dimensional provider; an unsupported graph fails construction. One closed coefficient DAG per graph retains the common (q[4],mu,epsilon) frame. Born and exact denominator kinematics use only its four physical momentum variables.

The native family projector applies complex Born weights and sums compatible helicities and graph terms before reduction. Its canonical columns are (q[4],mu2,epsilon). The auxiliary-free columns form N4; positive auxiliary columns form the dimensional remainder. Epsilon has zero tensor-rank weight and selects one of the reducer’s three epsilon sectors. The diagnostic reduces N4 and the remainder separately from the shared native coefficient buffer, never by subtracting integrated amplitudes. It exposes the resulting bare and R2 components separately. A future complete-only schedule may combine their sectors in a single reduction without changing the numerator construction.

Direct selection excludes every model R2 insertion. Source local UV and external-field half-Z factors are retained unchanged, including per-point renormalization scales and the six-internal-flavour/five-renormalized-flavour convention. The selection is fixed for the complete call. Model selection continues to use the ordinary four-dimensional program and source R2 trees.

The physical regression checks all three reference points against independently instrumented MadLoop bare, R2, local UV, external-field and complete values. It also compares the direct remainder with independently evaluated exact source R2 trees in double, DoubleFloat and 50-digit arbitrary arithmetic. The complete remainder’s poles are checked after summing all families; individual family poles are not discarded. Native empty batches and failure guards remain in place. Reference finite-amplitude comparisons themselves remain binary64 controls; the same-carrier R2 comparison is a separate precision check.

The binary64 diagnostic also accepts reduction_engine="fast-tensor" using NinjaRS b5b93be’s dense μ² numerator. Owner coefficient/layout APIs feed each existing HV epsilon sector without a scheme transformation. The complete gluon component and independent source-R2 controls also pass with this option. The original fast callback remains available; a sparse rank-five box timing did not show a speedup, so no automatic selection or performance claim follows. DoubleFloat/arbitrary arithmetic retain the generic reducer. Nonfinite dense coefficient-head errors remain batch errors and exclude this engine from the optional partial/rescue view.

The bounded direct gluon factory also accepts formal full/LC/NLC colour. Its UV scalar grades and rectangular overlaps are projected together, with one virtual degree P=5 across N4, the remainder, local UV and external fields; Born uses P=4. This does not infer a continuation of the excluded model R2 quartic rules. Other mixed-grade multi-term CTs require a separate incidence proof.

These controls do not enable public direct eligibility, general chiral or massive-gauge continuation, or a performance selection policy. Ordinary diagnostic evaluation still raises for a reduction failure in either channel; the optional bounded partial/rescue view is described below.

The internal full-colour QCD four-gluon model wrapper supports optional sparse verification/rescue with direct R2 fixed across every attempt. N4 and Delta reduction failures are collected before a common whole-point gather, preserving original IDs and previously integrated families. Both final sums are gathered together. Retried coefficient DAGs preserve physical/evanescent sectors and explicit epsilon powers; model R2 is never substituted during a direct retry. The native result records its executed strategy. Cross-precision agreement is empirical evidence under the configured bounds, including requested-carrier rounding, not independent certification of the reducer. This scope does not include source-owned direct kernels, CMS, formal colour or correlated views.
