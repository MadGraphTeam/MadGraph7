Standard UFO Spinor Order¶

The upstream SM Lorentz structures index each fermion pair in the reverse of the standard UFO order, e.g. FFV1 = Gamma(3,1,2) instead of MadGraph’s Gamma(3,2,1), and the importer compensated for it, which made standard UFO models wrong for off-shell fermion lines. sm-standard-ufo-spinor-order-v1 swaps the two fermion legs in the spinor slots of ufo/sm/lorentz.py and of the serialized JSON structures, so that they match the MadGraph SM; the importer reads the standard order.
