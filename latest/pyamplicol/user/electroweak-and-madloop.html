One-loop QCD+QED on loop_qcd_qed_sm, coming from MadLoop¶

pyAmpliCol ships H.-S. Shao’s two NLO UFO models with QCD and QED counterterms (arXiv:1804.10017) as UFOmodeldb ships them (commit 6ca4b120), with all their restriction cards, and with the model patch of UFOmodeldb issue 1 (V. Hirschi, gist, also posted on mg5amcnlo#475):

  • loop_qcd_qed_sm (alpha(M_Z) input scheme), as downloaded and patched;

  • loop_qcd_qed_sm_Gmu (G_mu input scheme), patched, as the mechanical Python-3 conversion of its Python-2 download (package-relative imports and .iteritems() -> .items() only; no declaration changes). The patch’s four eCoup_*_UV_EW.value[k] += '...' statements are folded into those counterterms’ constructors (same strings): the model loader certifies no value written after its constructor. Source checksums, the patch and the conversion are in src/pyamplicol/assets/models/PROVENANCE.toml.

The patch (1) splits the G0 t t~ R2 couplings R2_G0tt_{d,s,b} by loop content, [W+, G+, q] and [G+, q], keeping their sum (MadLoop, which inserts an R2 coupling only for a loop of exactly its label, now inserts them: its finite part of, e.g., t t~ > z h moves by up to 8 % and of t t~ > t t~ by up to 2.5 %; pyAmpliCol, which inserts every R2 vertex once, is unchanged); (2) makes the CKM matrix of the ckm restriction exactly unitary (standard parametrisation from the same four Wolfenstein inputs; it was truncated at O(lambda^3)); (3) restores the bottom-mass term of the G_mu charge counterterm (with a massive b, e.g. u d~ > t b~ moves by 9.5e-5 x Born and now equals Recola); (4) adds the missing SMINPUTS 1 line to the G_mu restrict_with_b_mass.dat. UFOmodeldb’s commit 60ee58bb took item (1) for the b quark; the rest is local to the shipped copies until UFOmodeldb takes it.

Retained limitation. The electroweak counterterms of both models omit the tau mass. With a massive tau (the ckm restriction has MTA = 1.777) the single pole of, e.g., u d~ > e+ ve keeps a residual MTA^2 / (2 sw^2 (s - MW^2)) x (alpha/2pi) x Born (about 3e-5 x (alpha/2pi) x Born at sqrt(s) = 500 GeV); MadLoop has the same. Fix MTA and ymtau to zero to remove it.

UFOmodeldb’s models (60ee58bb and the unpatched 6ca4b120) and the copies MG5_aMC’s online model database downloads (import model loop_qcd_qed_sm; they differ from UFOmodeldb’s in one coupling, R2_ZZHH, UFOmodeldb’s fix of the R2 vertices Z Z H H and Z Z G0 G0) take the same shipped [loop.model] profile, so pointing model.source at such a copy works too and reproduces MadLoop on it. An unpatched G_mu copy carries the defects the patch fixes (with a massive b, or on the ckm restriction); z z > h h-type processes differ between the download and UFOmodeldb’s model.

A request with electroweak (QED) or joint (QCD, QED) corrections runs on them with no [loop.model] section and no compatibility setting:

action = "generate"

[model]
source = "loop_qcd_qed_sm_Gmu"  # the shipped model; a path of that name wins

[process]
entries = [{ expression = "u u~ > e- e+", name = "dy" }]
loop_coupling_orders = ["QCD", "QED"]  # MG5_aMC: [virt=QCD QED]
coupling_order_policy = "minimal"

[loop.preparation]
fixed_parameters = { WT = "0", WZ = "0", WW = "0", WH = "0" }

[generation]
output = "artifacts/dy"

  • Scheme declaration. pyAmpliCol ships the declaration of both models (QCD and QED corrections, complete UV and R2 counterterms, ‘t Hooft-Veltman, on-shell electroweak renormalisation in the model’s input scheme, real on-shell masses) for the exact sources: it applies to a model whose UFO declarations have the recorded digest, which the shipped copy, the reference download and every restriction share. A modified copy (a massive-muon variant, edited parameters) has other declarations and needs its own [loop.model]. An explicit [loop.model] always wins.

  • Source repair. The models’ legacy object library drops the Goldstone flag of G- (the antiparticle of G+). Model preparation restores it by structure and records it (pyamplicol_source_repairs); the former compatibility = "gmu_particle_anti" is not needed and is still accepted.

  • Gauge. Born, loops and counterterms of an electroweak request take the ‘t Hooft-Feynman gauge of the models’ counterterms, with explicit Goldstones and ghosts.

  • Colour. Full colour by default, as MadLoop. Leading and next-to-leading colour are refused for these models (colour-declaration-coverage: no shipped N_c declaration covers their scheme).

  • Tree level. The same models serve tree requests (Born and real emission from one UFO).

  • Python 2. A Python-2 UFO (the raw G_mu download) is refused with ufo-python2, naming MG5_aMC’s conversion and the shipped model.

pyamplicol self-test evaluates an electroweak virtual (e- e+ > e- e+ [virt=QED] on loop_qcd_qed_sm) from a shipped prepared artifact and compares it with MadLoop.

MadLoop process syntax¶

MadLoop / MG5_aMC

pyAmpliCol

import model loop_qcd_qed_sm_Gmu-a0

[model] source = "loop_qcd_qed_sm_Gmu", restriction = "a0"

[virt=QCD QED], [virt=QED]

process.loop_coupling_orders = ["QCD", "QED"], ["QED"]

QED=2 (Born amplitude order)

process.max_coupling_orders = { QED = 2 }

QCD^2==4, QED^2<=4 (squared orders)

every squared-order sector is a separate partial result: read it out (result.for_order(...))

/ x

generation.particle_veto = ["x"]; a vetoed vector boson takes its ghosts. A veto that removes a loop species of a requested correction is refused on these models (veto-unlabelled-counterterms: their electroweak counterterms are not split by loop content), so photon-only QED is not expressible

set complex_mass_scheme True

not available yet (the complex-mass scheme is in progress); resonant widths are refused, see the recipes

!a! (a tagged photon)

[loop] external_photons ("final" by default, "all", "none" or 1-based legs): a tagged photon couples with alpha(0) with the conversion term, which needs restriction = "a0" on loop_qcd_qed_sm_Gmu; an untagged one keeps the loops’ coupling (MadLoop standalone’s raw values: "none"). See configuration

p p > e+ e- with a multiparticle label

the same expression: one artifact entry per subprocess with a Born; members without one are skipped, see below

MG5_aMC syntax inside a process expression is refused with madloop-syntax. MadLoop’s default order selection for processes with Born amplitudes of several orders drops the highest-alpha squared order unless the squared orders are given; pyAmpliCol returns every squared-order sector of the request’s limits, so compare sector by sector.

Multiparticle requests¶

A request with a multiparticle label generates its concrete processes that have a Born amplitude within the coupling-order limits. A member without one (g g > e+ e- in p p > e+ e-) is loop-induced. By default (process.loop_induced = "auto") it is skipped, with a typed record (key loop-induced) in the dry run (estimated_coverage.skipped_members) and in the artifact (manifest extension loop_skipped_members): the stored members’ sum is MadLoop’s Born x virtual. process.loop_induced = "include" also serves it, as |A_loop|^2 next to the Born members’ Born x virtual, each concrete process its own contribution (contribution = "loop-induced" or "virtual" in its results), never summed; this is the loop-squared contribution MadLoop’s multiparticle output holds. A request none of whose members has a Born is routed loop-induced (see Configuration; generated as |A_loop|^2), and refused (loop-induced) under process.loop_induced = "skip". p and j are the model’s massless quarks and gluon; add the photon with process.multiparticles.

Refusals¶

Every refusal is typed: the message starts with a listed key, names the rule and the remedy, and the exception carries key. pyamplicol generate --dry-run --json reports what the model and the Born need and lists every key with its remedy:

  • estimated_coverage.loop_configuration_refusals: run-card settings and model declarations (decided before any tree);

  • loop_admission_refusals: structures the generation refuses;

  • loop_internal_refusals: engine conditions met during generation;

  • native_export_refusals and process_request_refusals.

Key

a model with no [loop.model] that is not a shipped profile (a modified copy)

loop-model-corrections

corrections the declaration does not cover (QED on loop_sm)

direct-r2, fft-basis-trace, colour-selectors, relation-discovery, evaluator-backend, evaluator-tuning, covariant-multiplets
