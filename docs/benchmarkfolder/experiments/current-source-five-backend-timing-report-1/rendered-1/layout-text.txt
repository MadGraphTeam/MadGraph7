Five-backend Standard Model timings
  Descriptive shared-host measurements. These full-amplitude timings compare explicit CutTools, Ninja, COLLIER, NinjaRS and
  TIRust on the selected saved cohorts. There is no CPU reservation and no confidence-interval or final local-performance acceptance
  claim. The earlier 39 pages retain their separately bound scientific and timing history.


GNU Fortran (GCC) 15.2.0; AMD EPYC 9754 128-Core Processor; affinity CPU 12; one-thread environment on a shared, unreserved host.

Elapsed time is the unchanged Fortran SYSTEM_CLOCK wall clock, divided by the actual points × loops. Each backend has
ten position-balanced cycles (five Latin positions followed by reversal), with loops fixed after two retained calibrations.
The nominal batch target is two seconds; actual batch durations and counts remain in the closed evidence. The table
reports the median and unscaled median absolute deviation, not an error bar on the mean. Ratios are medians of
ten within-cycle backend / NinjaRS8 ratios. Actual timed batches range from 1.95 to 3.14 seconds; integer full-cohort
traversal counts can exceed the nominal target.
