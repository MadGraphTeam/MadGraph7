# Source-only CMS stress report supplement

Goal: prepare an auditable appendix showing the closed private CMS stress
outcomes and conditional universal QED pole comparisons, without changing
the current benchmark PDF, production Fortran or installed libraries.

The proposal reads scientific observations only from four explicitly pinned
closed public packets. The generator verifies their exact inventories and
payload bytes, joins eligible fixed-QP words to the closed amplitude parent,
checks duplicate answer orders, and derives eight pole table/plot rows.
Failed legacy coefficients remain opaque and are never decoded or used in
arithmetic. This is not a replacement for the packets' broader verifiers.

The proposed addition has a stage-status and reference-eligibility page,
followed by pole tables and the necessary assumptions and local-timing TODO.
Aim for two readable pages; allow a third rather than omit qualification
limits. An optional two-column pole ratio/residual figure has source only.
Its two physical points are not a stability distribution or statistical
benchmark. Point 1 is bootstrap; point 20 is the completed stress subset.

Retain the failed first build's `bytes`/`size` schema mismatch before native
execution. Retain the successor's partial stress timeout, three failed
TIRust workers and false candidate comparison gate. Legacy Ninja/NinjaRS
provide no valid numerical reference. Explain the QP interface observations
without claiming every native library's internal precision.

The two pole audits support only conditional coefficient consistency.

unobserved runtime coupling bits, incomplete Fortran rounding emulation,
reuse of earlier group-3 restored QP leaves without new candidate capture,
UV-renormalized full-ANS scope, and UV cancellation not separately tested.

stability and precise performance items remain open.
