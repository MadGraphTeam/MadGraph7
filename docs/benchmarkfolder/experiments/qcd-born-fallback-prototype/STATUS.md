The reviewed revision 2 remains a private prototype. Its source preparation and README describe the state at preparation; subsequent results are recorded separately below. No production generator was changed.

The Born-only qualification in `tree-native-2/` passed 147 ordinary helicity/polarization cases with identical result bits and IEEE flags, 25 repeated rescues of the original stress input, preservation of an entry invalid-operation flag, invalid-QP rejection, and rejection of finite QP output that overflows binary64 conversion. Both the original and fallback executables abort with SIGFPE in the original DP DHELAS routine when the caller enables the invalid-operation trap. The recovered Born value is `4.3074648942609752e19` after conversion to binary64.

`tree-native-1/` retains an unsuccessful test attempt. Its synthetic coupling was too small to cause the intended conversion overflow, so the test correctly stopped without validating that premise. The corrected test uses `GC_4=1e200`; no fallback source change was needed.

Seven alternating Born-only timing pairs on CPU 11 give a geometric wrapper/original ratio of 1.00293, with bootstrap interval [0.99526, 1.00977], and median paired added time of 0.435 microseconds. These measurements use the QCD n3 tree routine and a repeated ordinary point. They are diagnostic; they do not qualify the n1 whole-amplitude performance gate.

The actual original/prototype loop executables compiled and linked in `amplitude-native-1/`, using protected historical objects and libraries, and completed both backend-9 and backend-8 replays. Ordinary amplitudes before and after the stress point remain bit-identical, including accuracy, status and caller-visible flags. The stress Born is repaired, but both loop results remain rejected with status 420 and accuracy 1. Their loop coefficients equal the corresponding original rejected coefficients. The raw QP direction estimator is zero, while the host's per-reduction failure flag correctly overrides acceptance.

The expected double pole from the rescued Born is approximately `-7.010935615990818e18`. The returned rejected values are approximately `-4.58569e18`, a 34.59% discrepancy. The two rejected finite parts also disagree strongly. No checked-amplitude or physics-gate success is claimed. Sticky caller IEEE flags are recorded separately from the host's per-reduction failure flag; ordinary NinjaRS calls already leave an invalid flag in the original executable, and the prototype preserves that behavior exactly.

The independently prepared `host-edge-fixtures/` checks the actual initialization, cleanup and current-call certificate policy with explicit loop stubs. Revision 2 passes at O0 and O3; revision 1 reproduces both initialization/filter-publication and stale-H3 counterexamples. Wrapper fixtures cover rounding modes, flag merging and rejection behavior with meaningful negative controls. Those policy fixtures do not override the actual physical amplitude rejection.

The complete prototype remains unpromoted. A separate investigation must identify the common loop-level failure on the same inputs before this can qualify as automatic checked amplitude recovery. No production gate was weakened, input point repaired, or unchecked result accepted.
