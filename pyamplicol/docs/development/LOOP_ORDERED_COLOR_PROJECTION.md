# Complete history-family colour projection

> Historical: this describes the per-diagram legacy one-loop engine, removed on
> 2026-10-29 (MQ-LEGACY-REMOVE; v2 is the only one-loop engine, see
> `loop_v2/DESIGN.md` and §13.3 there). The modules, tools and tests named
> here may no longer exist.

Directed preparation and saved/public evaluation can project nonidentity colour
histories on actual formally continued model-rule owners. The saved descriptor
identifies each complete family and its sector degrees; it must agree across
ordinary and source-class preparations. The independently validated production
domain remains model-rule QCD, with the stable outgoing W+ control below.

For a fixed operation order and final typed colour-role set, the structural
leading degree comes from the complete compatible history family. It does not
depend on the requested subset of history pairs. The existing connection
catalogue supplies all histories, including original-gluon splitting through
its opt-in `include_physical_splittings` option; the default tree soft catalogue
is unchanged. Physical labels are fixed, while created final roles and retired
auxiliary labels use the existing shared chronology/canonicalization rules.

Each Born squared-order sector has one degree. Each virtual squared-order
sector has one degree across all ordered family pairs, bare loops, UV, model
R2 and external-field insertions. Counterterm rows carry their actual scalar
colour grades, active tensor masks and amplitude orders before projection.
LC keeps degree P; NLC keeps P, P−1 and P−2 with fixed Nf. The numerical Nc=3
substitution and averaging occur afterward. Event values, helicities, epsilon
coefficients and source vectors cannot change this degree.

`PreparedDirectedColorBank` references the actual complete prepared owner and
its existing ordinary `FormalColorMetrics`. The ordinary metrics are unchanged.
If the selected histories contain identity, its exact Born/loop matrices and
degrees must equal the ordinary formal owner; existing bound CT/field weights
provide an additional consistency check. That latter check does not reconstruct
an arbitrary-Nc counterterm from its Nc=3 value.

The bank is shared across ordinary, singleton and joint source preparations.
Arithmetic rebinding checks existing owner, tensor/order, degree and averaging
metadata without repeating colour algebra. The source contracts, phases and
spectator layouts retain their established ownership rules. Forward and swapped
history matrices remain distinct; the second term is conjugated after reduction.

The cold controls include a suppressed dipole that must not be promoted by an
isolated request, a Born–loop direction with zero ordinary and diagonal overlap
but nonzero cross history, exact charge row sums, graded UV/R2/field rows and
zero sectors. A rectangular two-Born/three-loop, two-emission non-Hermitian
control compares each coefficient with the independent tree contraction path
and detects replacing the observable by twice one real part. Catalogue controls
cover original/auxiliary gluon splitting and dependent emission order; exact
previous-module catalogue JSON/IDs are unchanged through order three.

The retained complete stable outgoing W+ QCD owner supplies a physical control
without new graph generation. Its identity, complete one-emission dipoles and
physical-gluon split exercise every complete component and all Laurent
coefficients. This one-colour physical owner supplements the rectangular cold
oracle; it does not independently certify a nonzero anti-Hermitian observable.

The cold artifact is evaluated in double, DoubleFloat and 70-digit arbitrary
precision against retained producer results, covering every component and both
directed terms for ordinary, W-source, gluon-source and joint-source classes.
Typed-history corruption controls check active labels, fresh labels, physical
gluon retirement, family identity and degree axes. Labels remain opaque; the
controls include the maximum unsigned native point identifier.

Public LC/NLC artifacts preserve contracted colour shape (one component),
coupling-order and Laurent axes, grouped histories and the three declared
source classes. A scale that underflows in double triggers recovery only for
the failing lane from its original exact inputs. Empty/tail reuse and `clear`
retain the same observable contract. One standalone binary64 Rust client checks
the actual artifact, source scaling, owned history/source labels and shape;
this is a transport check, not another independent physics reference. Ordinary
LC resolved artifacts retain their separate signed canonical-component
requirements.

Projected flavour reuse requires its own exact continuation proof, including
the actual directed family/degree/bank and source contracts. It is not inferred
from numerical equality at Nc=3. No operation-count or family-size limit is
introduced by this preparation.
