Scope

The licence above covers the project's own code and data: everything in this
repository except the third-party content listed below. It includes the scalar
masters generated from oneloopmaster's expressions,
crates/iregi-masters/src/generated/{a0,b0,db0,c0,d0,constants}.rs, which
transcribe Andreas van Hameren's OneLOop; his permission to distribute and
relicense that transcription was received by Valentin Hirschi by email
(recorded 2026-11-06).

Third-party content (not covered by the licence above; each keeps its own
licence and notices):

- dependencies/feynkit-integrals: code extracted from FeynKit
  (https://github.com/alphal00p/gammaloop, revision 865afdc4), MIT OR
  Apache-2.0 as declared by its manifest; upstream licence text in
  dependencies/feynkit-integrals/LICENSE, provenance in its UPSTREAM.md.
- dependencies/linnest and dependencies/linnest-patches: linnest from
  https://github.com/alphal00p/gammaloop (revision 9102f885), MIT as
  declared by its manifest;
  notices in dependencies/linnest/UPSTREAM_REPOSITORY_LICENSE,
  dependencies/linnest/typst/LICENSE (MIT) and
  dependencies/linnest/layout/native/LICENSE.networkx (BSD-3-Clause, NetworkX).
- python/tirust/data/render/fonts: the unchanged STIX Two Math font from
  gammaloop 9102f885 (crates/linnet-render-py/fonts), SIL Open Font License
  1.1; licence text in python/tirust/data/licenses/STIX-Two-OFL.txt.
- python/tirust/data/licenses: the licence texts of the bundled or linked
  components (the font, FeynKit, Symbolica), reproduced verbatim.
- python/tirust/_types/symbolica/community/{tensor,graph,hepkit}/__init__.pyi:
  type-only stubs mirrored by scripts/vendor-python-type-stubs.py with only
  their import paths rewritten (sources and hashes in
  python/tirust/owner-stubs.json), from gammaloop 9102f885 (tensor: spynso3,

- fixtures/models/sm.json: copied unchanged from gammaloop 865afdc4

- Evidence under validation/ and docs/research/ that is not the project's own

and crates.io packages) are not part of this repository and carry their own
licences; Symbolica is distributed under its own licence
